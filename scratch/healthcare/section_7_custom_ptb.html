Our Healthcare App Development Process

Healthcare software doesn't move in a straight line. Compliance, clinical input, and engineering happen at the same time, not one after another.

01

Requirement Gathering

We collaborate with clinical and non-clinical teams to map workflows, uncover pain points, and establish well-defined technical and functional requirements.

02

Healthcare Compliance by Design

Our methodology embeds the standards of HIPAA, HITECH, and FHIR into the design to enable security, compliance, and interoperability.

03

UI/UX Design

We deliver user-centric designs, wireframes, and clickable prototypes based on user requirements and processes, along with accessibility testing in compliance with WCAG 2.1.

04

Development

We employ Agile sprints and best coding practices to build your solution whilst ensuring its security with test driven development.

05

Security Testing

We perform penetration tests, vulnerability scans, and security reviews to uncover potential risks and mitigate them prior to production.

06

Release

Our release process covers staging, user acceptance testing, and production deployment, including validation of backup and rollback procedures.

07

Maintenance & Support

We offer continuous monitoring, security patches, troubleshooting, and new features to adapt to evolving requirements and keep the app dependable.
